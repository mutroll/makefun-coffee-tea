## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib only, deterministic, no network, no randomness)
writes exactly one character — `A` for coffee — to `entry.txt`, nothing else.

Coffee is the bolder, more vivid stance: the full-bodied claim that lands as
a whole answer on its own, funnier in its morning swagger, and truer as the
drink with the stronger claim on the day.
